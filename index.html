Explore the Biomes

Learn about the different ecosystems that make up our planet and the animals that call them home.

Tropical Rainforest

Warm, wet, and home to over half of all of the world's plants and animal species.

Savanna

Grasslands with scattered trees, famous for large herbivores and predators.

Oceans

Covers around 70% of our world. This is where all life started from.

Tundra

Very cold places where animals like polar bears and arctic foxes live.

Desert

Dry Areas usually filled with sand. Did you know Antarctica is a desert?

Broadleaf Forest

Forests with four distinct seasons, home to bears, deer and owls.

Wetlands

Swamps and marshes, vital for water purification.

Chaparral

Shrubland plant community with a Mediterranean climate of mild, wet winters and hot, dry summers.
